Safety & Policies

Building a trustworthy community for collectors.

Draft community standards: These rules describe expected conduct. Transaction protection, payouts, refunds and dispute procedures are not yet guaranteed services and will be published before paid checkout launches.

Community Guidelines

  • Treat other members respectfully. No harassment, hate, threats, or bullying.
  • Use your own accurate listing photos. Do not present AI-generated or stock photos as the actual item.
  • Describe condition, alterations, damage, missing parts, and whether packaging has been opened or resealed.
  • Never sell stolen, counterfeit, or deceptively represented items. Do not misrepresent proxies, reproductions, graded cards, or sealed products.
  • No spam, fake reviews, impersonation, deceptive offers, or attempts to manipulate transactions.
  • No weapons, illegal drugs, hazardous items, adult-only products, or other unlawful or restricted goods.
  • Respect accepted offers and do not promise the same unique item to multiple buyers.
  • Never send phishing links or request passwords, one-time codes, or sensitive financial information in messages.

Reporting and enforcement

Use the in-app report and block tools for suspected scams, prohibited items, spam, or harassment. DeckPlay may review reports and remove content, restrict accounts, or suspend users according to severity. A report is not proof of wrongdoing. Appeals and support processes are being developed.

Buyer and seller safety

Keep messages and transaction records on DeckPlay when possible. Do not ship based on screenshots claiming payment. Verify actual order status. Never share payment card details or passwords in chat. Before paid transactions launch, DeckPlay will publish separate rules covering payment processing, shipping deadlines, cancellations, refunds, claims, trades, and seller payouts.

Privacy and account information

DeckPlay uses account information and listing data to operate the marketplace. A complete Privacy Policy describing data collection, retention, sharing, user rights, and account deletion is in preparation and must be published before public commercial launch. Do not post personal addresses or private information publicly.

Legal documents still required

Formal Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, payment terms, prohibited-items details, and buyer/seller protection terms are being prepared. This page is not a substitute for those documents or a promise of payment protection.
