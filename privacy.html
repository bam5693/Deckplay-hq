This page is an initial explanation of data handling, not a complete final privacy notice.

Information used

DeckPlay uses account credentials through its authentication provider, profile information, listings and images, messages, offers, reports, and technical service data to operate the marketplace.

Service providers

DeckPlay currently uses third-party infrastructure such as Supabase and Cloudflare. Payment and shipping partners will be identified before those features go live. Never post sensitive financial details in chat.

Access and retention

Users should only share information needed for a transaction. Detailed retention schedules, deletion procedures, user rights, and support contact information are being finalized. Do not treat account deletion as implemented until verified.

Security

Use a unique password and never share verification codes. Report suspected unauthorized access through the available reporting tools.
