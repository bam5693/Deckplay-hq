## Research protocol
- Each row tracks source, date checked, what the site **advertises/documents**, what was **actually observed**, a product implication, follow-up, and T9 objective. Avoid presenting a help article as a firsthand completed transaction.
- Geographic differences: Cardmarket example is an EU-language/currency market; eBay and TCGplayer pages describe US flows; PSA/Upper Deck product history may cover Canada or other eras. Do not transfer protection, fees, or set nomenclature across regions.
- Popularity is **not established** by appearing in a catalog. A product being available or an official set existing is not evidence it is a top seller.

### DeckPlay CURRENT staging comparison (static-source audit, T9.1/T9.3)

Source: [current staging index.html](https://github.com/bam5693/Deckplay-hq/blob/test/staging-reporting/index.html) (read 2026-11-01, commit above).

- Seller `sportsSport`: Football, Basketball, Baseball, Hockey, Soccer, Racing, Wrestling, Other. Seller `sportsBrand` options: **Topps, Panini, Upper Deck, Fleer, Donruss, Bowman, Score, Other / Unknown**. **O-Pee-Chee is not an option**; there is no dedicated exact sports set or parallel field in the sports section. Seller `sportsBrand` is **required** for sports cards. When seller chooses Other/Unknown, the structured `Brand:` line is literally `Other / Unknown`; they may explain real brand in description, but that is not equivalent to searchable structured brand.
- Buyer `marketSportsBrand` is a free-text filter and `sportsListingDetail(item,"Brand")` matches the stored structured `Brand:` (or updated `Sports brand:`) with lowercase substring comparison. Therefore buyer filtering by `O-Pee-Chee` **can miss** a correctly described vintage card that had to be saved as `Other / Unknown` (unless later edited to structured custom brand, if supported). This is a **source-based risk**, not a demonstrated live regression.
- Seller TCG `tcgEra`/`tcgSet` are progressive and optional, with custom set/variant fields; Pokémon includes `Phantasmal Flames`, One Piece includes OP/EB/PRB/ST release families. Preserve that good fallback and don't recreate an all-in-one giant dropdown.
- Seller has `listingType` separate from `category` and buyer sports brand/year/player filters. Do not conclude any previously reported duplicate-category screen defect is still present without signed-in current-staging verification.
- Current signed-in seller and buyer acceptance was **not** executed during this advisory run. The code inspection does not establish mobile usability, backend search parity, or actual staging deployment SHA.
