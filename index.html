New to DeckPlay? Start here.

Find and share trading cards, sports memorabilia, video games, consoles, controllers, accessories and other collectibles. No purchase is required to browse.

  1. Find: Browse Marketplace, search by name, or filter by category and game.
  2. Save: Sign in and open a listing to add it to your Saved items.
  3. Contact: Open a listing to message the collector; check their profile and item details first.
  4. Negotiate: Where enabled, send a price offer or propose a trade and follow replies in Inbox.
  1. Join: Sign in or create a collector account; choose Sell an item.
  2. Describe: Pick the correct category, condition, quantity and price. Include exact variants or deck contents when relevant.
  3. Photograph: Add real photos of the item and review the listing before publishing.
  4. Respond: Check Inbox for questions, price offers and trades. Only agree to terms you can fulfill.

Beta limitation: Offers and trades are proposals only. Checkout, payment, payouts, purchased shipping and shipping labels are disabled. Never send payment or address details through a demo checkout.

    Create a marketplace listing

    Add the details, choose your best cover photo, then publish. Photos are required.

    Include quantities and card numbers so buyers and traders know exactly what is included.

    Use explicit quantities such as 4x Card name. Quantity checks do not verify game rules or deck completeness.

    Missing a card, set or variant in the choices? Describe it here. Include rarity, language, edition, foil treatment, serial number and flaws. For mixed lots, describe each card’s variant and condition. Your asking price is yours to set.

    Selling a mixed lot? List each included item, its quantity, condition, and any notable variants in the description so buyers know exactly what they receive.

    Exact printing / custom card details

    Use the exact text on your card. A custom set or variant replaces the corresponding choice above.

    Skip any detail you do not know. Use the description for serial numbers, condition notes and anything else collectors should see.

    Required for sports cards: sport, brand, year, and player or subject. Set and parallel details are optional.

    Use Near Mint for clean cards with only minimal wear. Graded cards require the grading company and grade.

    What is included

    Everything is sold together for the listing price, not priced per copy. Count complete decks or sealed products as units; use deck details for cards inside a deck. If you list item-by-item contents, start each line with a quantity such as 2x; the total must match the quantity included. Describe each item's exact variant, condition and any missing parts.
