Policy preview — not an active purchase-protection guarantee

Community GuidelinesTermsPrivacyBuyer & Seller Safety

Payments and protected checkout have not been launched. This page describes proposed rules that must be matched to the eventual payment processor.

Buyers should inspect item descriptions and photos; sellers should disclose flaws, reseals, alterations, counterfeit concerns, and graded-card certification information. Do not pay or ship based on a chat screenshot.

Shipping and delivery

Future checkout must clearly state shipping costs, tracking, handling deadlines, delivery confirmation, lost-package procedures, and who bears each risk.

Claims and disputes

A future claim process must define eligible issues, evidence, response windows, refunds, appeals, and the payment provider's chargeback rules. No 48-hour inspection or payout hold is guaranteed yet.

Trades

Trading requires its own protection design. Until that system is implemented and verified, users should not assume DeckPlay insures or guarantees exchanged items.
