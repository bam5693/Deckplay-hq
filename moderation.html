    Incident handoff · read-only

    Prepare a private, customer-data-free escalation when a service or queue is unavailable. This page does not create an incident, change feature controls, or execute a rollback.

    Manual incident and rollback checklist
    1. Confirm the affected environment in its own trusted dashboard; staging and production are separate.
    2. Pause further releases and feature changes until the owner decides how to proceed.
    3. Escalate the safe summary to the owner without customer report contents, account identifiers or secrets.
    4. Before any production rollback, independently verify current database, Auth and Storage backups and a rehearsed recovery path. Obtain explicit owner approval.
    5. After recovery, verify the affected customer journey and record the outcome outside this page.

    No feature switches, bans, payments, shipping, backup operations or rollback actions are available here.
