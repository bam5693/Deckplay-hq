# DeckPlay HQ — Competitive Advantage and Launch Readiness

Goal: build a leading nationwide buy/sell/trade collectibles marketplace, prioritizing superior user experience, trust, value and active marketplace liquidity over feature count.

## Cross-cutting acceptance criteria for all eight roadmap trunks
1. Core workflow works reliably with automated and realistic manual testing.
2. Compare usability and friction against relevant competitors.
3. Demonstrate concrete collector value and accessible mobile experience.
4. Evaluate safety, transparent total fees, shipping options, privacy and scalability.
5. Exercise buyer, seller, trader and collector scenarios using Charles, Kyle, Tanner and Jayden test personas; report evidence and gaps.

## Ninth workstream: competitive intelligence and launch readiness
- Maintain a dated, evidence-based comparison of CardSwap, Cardly Market, Rippr, TCG Traders, CardSynced and other relevant platforms; distinguish advertised from tested capabilities.
- Compare trading, offers, cards-plus-cash, collections, deck builders, want lists, alerts, search, inbox, trust, payments, shipping, fee transparency, mobile UX and seller onboarding.
- Identify differentiators without copying blindly: optional shareable collections with contact/privacy controls, trade matching, conversation-based negotiations, low-cost single-card shipping and clear pre-checkout totals.
- Define launch gates for secure transactions, buyer/seller protections, dispute handling, operational readiness, usability, and performance.
- Track acquisition, active listings, active buyers/sellers, successful transactions, repeat use, conversion, dispute rate and support load once real users arrive.
- Prioritize core marketplace reliability and safe staged rollout before expanding scope. No claims of superiority without evidence.

## Execution boundaries
This is a roadmap addition, not authorization to modify production. Continue development and testing only on test/staging-reporting, isolated staging Supabase and private staging Cloudflare Worker. Do not modify deckplayhq.com or real user data. Keep draft PR #2 as review boundary. Avoid unnecessary CI minutes and nonessential scope expansion.
